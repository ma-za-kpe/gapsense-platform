# Curriculum Evidence and Limitations White Paper

Last reviewed: 2026-07-30

The public, same-origin version of this disclosure is `/evidence`.

## Purpose and Disclaimer

This paper describes the GapSense evidence boundary. It is not an endorsement by NaCCA, NCDC, a
ministry, an examination authority, or an educator-review body.

## Public and Local Boundaries

Local development represents all 176 researched Ghana and Uganda catalogue cells and uses
page-traced machine-extracted trees for 170 cells. Six Uganda cells remain authority-confirmed
without complete public syllabus bytes.

Production intentionally exposes the catalogue and sanitized source ledger, but no private PDFs,
substantial official text, or private normalized projections. A public missing state is a
publication boundary, not a claim that the curriculum area does not exist.

## Known Issues

- Machine extraction is not educator review or pedagogical validation.
- Source custody and checksums are not permission to redistribute.
- Affected OCR glyphs and complex table order need exact page reconciliation.
- All extracted trees need scoped country, subject, educator, cultural, and rights review.
- Illustrative assessment samples are not curriculum-aligned diagnostics.
- GapSense is not an official curriculum or examination authority.

## Contribution and Correction Policy

Contributors can help obtain unchanged official bytes or written authority transmissions,
introduce qualified reviewers, arrange rights review, or report exact source-page defects. Reports
should name country, phase, level, subject, release ID, source ID, page, observed behavior, expected
behavior, and non-restricted supporting evidence.

Material corrections require reproducible evidence, deterministic rematerialization, paired tests,
updated hashes, reviewed public/private boundaries, hosted CI success, and a versioned release.
Restricted curricula and learner information must not be placed in public issues.
